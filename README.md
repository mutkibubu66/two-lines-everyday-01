# Everyday Things, Two Lines — The Toaster

[![make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479)](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479)

## The two lines

> A toaster is a tiny sunbathing bed for bread: it lies down under hot orange wires until its sides turn golden, then pops up like it's had enough of the beach.
>
> The sunbed part maps — heat from above, browning both sides, popping up when done — but bread can't choose to leave early or ask for shade, and no toaster ever gets a tan line.

Line 1 explains a toaster to a 10-year-old through a concrete analogy:
bread sunbathing under hot wires until it turns golden and pops up. Line 2
is honest about the analogy: it names what the sunbed maps (heat from
above, browning both sides, popping up when done) and what it leaves out
(bread has no choice, no shade, and no tan line).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (two lines, under 400
characters total, valid UTF-8) and prints them to stdout. Pure standard
library, no network.
